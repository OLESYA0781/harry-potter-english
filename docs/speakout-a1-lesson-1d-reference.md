## Grammar Bank 1D (printed p.95)
- a/an before singular count nouns and adjective + singular noun; **sound**, not first letter, determines a vs an: a phone, an actor, a university, a UK passport.
- plural formation: -s (books, keys), -es after ch/sh/s/ss/x (watches, buses, glasses, dishes, boxes), consonant+y -> -ies (countries, universities). No a/an before plurals.
- the for a unique/contextually identified object (the teacher, the board); keep brief, as lesson focuses a/an.
- have/has: I/you/we/they have; he/she/it has.
- **Practice 1** a/an with: photo, restaurant, Indian name, nationality, phone number, address, shop assistant, American city, taxi driver, sandwich. Correct: a, a, an, a, a, an, a, an, a, a.
- **Practice 2** plural forms: photos, restaurants, Indian names, nationalities, phone numbers, addresses, shop assistants, American cities, taxi drivers, sandwiches.
- **Practice 3** eight picture-based sentence completions; first four start 'I have…', next four 'My city has…'; supplied first example 'two bags'. Original illustrations are not available in this text reference; use newly created unambiguous scenes with checked counts and answers rather than inventing original picture details.

## Lesson 1D continued (printed p.15)
- **5A** list at least five items in one's bag/purse/wallet. **5B** review useful phrases: describing number and contents; stating no bag but things in pocket; admitting 'I don't know the name in English'.
- **6A** pair conversation about what is in their bags and count shared objects. **6B** report former partner's bag using third-person has. **6C** say an object everyone has. For one student, use two prepared illustrated bags to compare, plus spoken interview with teacher. Suggested fixed bags: Emma phone/notebook/two pens/three keys/book; Harry phone/notebook/banana/umbrella/wallet. Common: phone + notebook. Add accessible tap-to-select fallback for drag and drop.
- **7A** match two lost/found posts to two private messages: post 1 (Evi: lost small black purse with card and ~€5 on High Street) -> message b (finder has purse); post 2 (Jim: found key ring with three keys in Adastra Park) -> message a (owner claims keys). **7B** write a lost post about one item. **7C** write private message claiming to have found partner's object. **7D** read and match classmates' posts and messages; adapt to individual teaching with additional original examples.
